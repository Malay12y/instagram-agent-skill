# Social MCP and Comment-to-DM — Ocean Calling (2026-10-09)
1. Start with the existing Notion Instagram DM & Comment Automation SOP (https://app.notion.com/p/3df148a635d381949f45eedbb1ad19ba). Its keyword triggers, approved messages, opt-in and handoff rules remain authoritative.
2. Proposed MCP 'BlowPotato' from an online video is unverified. Do not connect business accounts, grant permissions, or claim a working comment-to-DM service without official product evidence and an end-to-end test.
3. Validate providers against Meta's approved Instagram APIs, account authorization scopes, triggers, duplicate prevention, message delivery, rate limits, data policy, auditability, unsubscribe expectations, and pricing. ManyChat and LinkDM remain existing alternatives, not automatically replaced.
4. Flow: approved post/reel -> comment keyword -> permitted DM with matching retreat information -> tracked reply and link -> qualified lead into authorized CRM -> human response for booking, refunds, complaints or animal welfare.
5. Allowed sample keywords: WHALE, MANTA, FREEDIVE, DATES, PRICE, WAITLIST. Confirm dates and offers are currently approved before generating any message.
6. Metrics: post ID, trigger keyword, DM attempts, successful deliveries, responses, clicks, qualified enquiries and attributable confirmed bookings. Mark missing fields rather than invent counts.
7. Human approval for posts, outgoing sensitive replies, new campaigns and provider account connection. Never scrape Instagram user profiles or send unsolicited bulk DMs.
